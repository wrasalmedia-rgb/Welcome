# WRASAL MEDIA

**A computational film studio native to cyberspace.**

We explore filmmaking as a system of persistent worlds, creative intelligence, software-defined production, and digital craft.

A set is more than an image. A character is more than a prompt. A film studio can exist as computation—not simply as a virtual copy of a physical one.

## What we're exploring

| Track | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| **Cinematic systems** | Tools and workflows for development, production, editorial, and world continuity. |
| **Persistent worlds** | Reusable environments with coherent geography, history, materials, and rules. |
| **Creative intelligence** | Governed tools for assisting human creative decisions without replacing their authority. |
| **Language & memory** | Query, provenance, and rule-language research for creative and operational systems. |
| **Story projects** | Narrative worlds and experiments, including DUST, FUNUJĒ, and YUTE. |

These tracks describe the studio's development and research interests; they do not imply public releases or production-ready software.

## Principles

- **Worlds, not one-off outputs.** Build places and systems that can be revisited and revised.
- **People decide.** Computational assistants support creative agency; they do not silently assume authority.
- **Evidence and imagination stay distinguishable.** A design proposal is not a verified implementation.
- **Respect boundaries.** Public artifacts are separated from private research, source code, personnel information, and client data.
